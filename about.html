About
Paige 18

Small stories, told with care. Est. 2020 · Vancouver, BC

PAIGE 18 Production is an independent company making film, theatre and web series in Vancouver. We believe the stage and the screen are one craft — both begin with a room, a few people and a story worth staying for.

The name comes from our first production: eighteen short monologues by eighteen local writers, staged in a community hall on a rainy weekend. Everything we have made since grew out of that room — the collaborators, the audience, and the idea that small, specific stories travel furthest.

Today we develop and produce original work across three formats, partner with festivals, broadcasters and theatres across Canada, and run an open development room for emerging writers and directors on the West Coast.
