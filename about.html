Paige 18 Productions is a boutique production company founded by Canadian actress, writer, producer and director Rhonda Dent.

Dedicated to storytelling across film, television and theatre, Paige 18 Productions develops and produces original projects that entertain, inspire and connect audiences.

With a passion for authentic, heartfelt stories and creative collaboration, the company brings ideas to life through independent filmmaking, live theatre and innovative visual storytelling, championing meaningful narratives and the power of creativity.

About
me

Rhonda Dent is a six-time Leo-nominated Canadian actress, writer, producer, director, children's author and photographer with over 25 years of professional experience.

She has appeared in numerous film and television productions, including leading roles in Lifetime Movies of the Week. A graduate of the University College of the Fraser Valley's theatre program and Langara College's Film Arts Directing program, Rhonda is the founder of Paige 18 Productions, where she develops original film, television and theatre projects.

Passionate about meaningful storytelling, she is currently developing Footprints, a feature-length Sasquatch adventure mystery set in the Pacific Northwest.
